    How the estimates work

    VDOT is calculated from the average pace of each week's quality reps, treated as about maximal-aerobic effort. Race estimates are the Daniels race-time equivalent of that VDOT multiplied by an endurance factor per distance (half marathon ×1.136, 10K ×1.052, 5K ×1.014). The factors were calibrated to the 14 Oct estimates and are adjusted each Wednesday from long runs and tempo sessions. Earlier points use the same factors and are marked retrospective.
